01 / ABOUT ME

A different kind of developer.

My background is in dentistry, but my interest in technology started long before I began clinical practice. Since 2017, I've explored software development, AI and technology-driven projects.

I like understanding a problem before jumping into the code. That might mean building a website, working with a dataset, experimenting with a machine-learning model or thinking through how technology could improve healthcare delivery.

Outside individual software projects, I founded RobotProf and work on initiatives that introduce young people to technology through Kids in LET. These experiences have shaped how I think about building products: they should solve actual problems, not just look good in a demo.

I'm currently growing my software portfolio and looking for opportunities where I can contribute, learn from other developers and build useful products.

What I bring: a healthcare background, curiosity about how systems work, experience building initiatives from scratch and a willingness to learn by doing.
