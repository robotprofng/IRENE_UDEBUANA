A LITTLE ABOUT ME

So, what's my story?

I studied dentistry, but technology has been part of my life since 2017. I've spent time learning software development, exploring AI and building initiatives around technology education.

I don't want to write code just for the sake of writing code. I want to understand a problem, figure out what might help, build something and see whether it actually works.

That's part of why I started RobotProf and Kids in LET. Both started with a simple question: how can we make technology useful and accessible to the people who need it?

I'm now building my software portfolio, strengthening my technical skills and looking for opportunities to work with people who enjoy making useful things too.

Outside my laptop, I'm usually working in dentistry, thinking about healthcare robotics, planning a technology project or figuring out how to make an idea sustainable.
